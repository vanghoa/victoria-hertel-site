**Key words: material circularity, aromatic habitats, fragrance perception**  
<br />
*Nocturne* consists of hand-blown glass scent vessels repurposed from the tempered float glass panels of the Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix Night Race floodlights. The original panels were broken down, re-blown via a hot glass process, and ground to fit sealing perfume stoppers. Each vessel contained a 
custom scent composed by a local craft fragrance lab to evoke race track aromas, creating a sensory bridge between the track and the off-site setting at The Arts House.
<br />
Venturing beyond traditional botanical notes, the work explores aromatic territories like butyl rubber, smoke, and octane, highlighting the myriad scent traces subtly shaping our sensory perception. While F1 is renowned for its audio-visual impact, *Nocturne* emphasises the role of scent in processing, interpreting, and engaging with our surroundings, serving as a tribute to olfactory ephemerality.
<br />
<br />
**Close-Up [ Hot Glass ]**  
The haptic glass textures resulted from 
re-fusing the Formula 1 floodlight shards 
through a hot glass roll-up at 900°C. 
Upcycling pre-existing tempered glass 
is both physically taxing and materially 
challenging due to its stiffness. To 
become malleable, it requires a long time 
to heat up, yet it cools down fairly quickly, 
allowing only brief moments to re-shape 
the glass between temperature flashes.

**Detail [ Perfume Grit ]**  
To preserve the quality of the added 
scents and prevent evaporation, the 
vessels required hermetic stoppers. 
However, since each vessel was 
handmade and slightly different, off-the
shelf fragrance stoppers were ineffective. 
Employing traditional perfume bottle 
crafting techniques, I created custom 
stoppers, manually grinding each glass 
stopper in with silicon carbide grit.

**Overview [ Octane Mimicry ]**  
Each of the bottled scents was custom
synthesized by a Singaporean craft 
frangrance lab to mimic unconventional 
aromas. By trying to match or find overlaps 
with these unfamiliar fragrances using 
scent combinations from their memories, 
visitors could playfully engage with their 
sense of smell and remembrance.
